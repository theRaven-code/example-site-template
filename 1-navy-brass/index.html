About the Group

Four operating companies, one standard of work.

Founded in Singapore in 2007, Winner Group began in marine and offshore outfitting and has grown into civil, infrastructure and industrial works through Winner Construction & Engineering, established in 2010.

Our management team and skilled workforce complete every project safely, efficiently and in accordance with Singapore standards and client requirements.
